Investor Business Plan

This investor-ready summary outlines how AgriSolve will become profitable in 4 years through diversified income and regenerative farming.

Executive Summary

AgriSolve integrates livestock, crops, and tourism on a 16-hectare regenerative farm. The goal is to earn R345K–R490K in net profit by Year 4 through diversified, phased, and low-debt operations.

Objectives

  • Secure 16 hectares of land
  • Reach R600K+ annual net profit within 4 years
  • Keep fixed overheads and debt low
  • Deliver investor ROI of 20–30% p.a.

Income Streams

  • Sheep, Bees, Broilers
  • Glamping, Picnics, Horse Rides
  • Farm Shop/Upsells

4-Year Rollout Plan

  • Year 1: 70 ewes, 10 hives, glamping unit, broilers
  • Year 2: Expand broilers & veggies
  • Year 3: Horse rides & workshops
  • Year 4: Scale bees, open farm shop

Infrastructure Needs

Total Capital Needed: R1.6M – R1.9M, including fencing, solar, greywater, and working capital.

Marketing & Sales

  • CSA subscription model for meat & honey
  • Tourism platforms like Airbnb
  • Local collaborations, content marketing

Investor Offer

Up to 1,500 shares are offered at R500 each, raising R750,000 in equity. As the business grows, the value of each share is expected to increase. Investors can expect a target ROI of 20–30% per year from Year 4, paid through dividends based on company profits. After 5 years, you can choose to receive dividends, reinvest returns, hold your shares, or sell them back to the company—potentially earning more than your original investment.

Investor FAQ

Q: What is the share price and total equity offered?
A: Up to 1,500 shares are available at R500 each, raising a total of R750,000 in equity.

Q: When do returns start?
A: From Year 4, with a target annual return of 20–30%, typically paid through dividends based on company profits.

Q: How much are the dividends per share?
A: Based on projected returns, dividends could range from R100 to R150 per share annually in Years 4 and 5, depending on business performance.

Q: Will my share increase in value?
A: Yes. As the company grows and becomes more profitable, the market value of each share is expected to increase beyond the original R500 price.

Q: How can I get my money back?
A: After 5 years, you may choose to continue holding your shares, reinvest your returns, receive ongoing dividends, or sell your shares back to the company—potentially at a higher value than your original investment.

Q: How does the company pay returns?
A: Returns come from profits generated by the farm through the sale of products like meat, wool, and dairy. These profits fund dividends and share buybacks.

Q: What happens if I don’t want to sell my shares?
A: You can continue to hold your shares beyond 5 years and earn dividends as long as the company remains profitable.

Risks & Mitigation

  • Drought: Irrigated camps, water storage
  • Market Access: Direct digital and physical sales
  • Load Shedding: Solar, gas, and cold storage
